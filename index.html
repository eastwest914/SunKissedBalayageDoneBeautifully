The Short Version

Who's Behind Honeybliss Salon

Honeybliss Salon opened on the Upper East Side around a simple belief: highlights should look like they happened in the sun, not under foil. Every color consultation starts with understanding your hair's history before any formula is mixed.

That same thoughtful approach carries through to every cut, blowout and finishing touch we send clients out the door with.

We see clients maintaining long-standing blonde or balayage color alongside first-timers looking to go lighter for the first time, giving both the same careful consultation.

We're located on Lexington Ave and open Monday through Friday, 10am to 8pm, and Saturdays, 10am to 7pm.

Balayage, Highlights & Full-Service Styling
